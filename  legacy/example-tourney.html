KAWPA BASS on the BAY 11

Erie, PA, United States
May 2nd 06:00 am - May 2nd 02:30 pm 2026, Eastern • 1 Day Tourney

--Search Angler--

Presque Isle Bay

Welcome to Presque Isle Bay, rich in history and world famous for its Smallmouth Bass fishing! Presque Isle Bay offers Largemouth and Smallmouth Bass in abundance which means this event will be action packed!

Bass on the Bay 11 will be Kayak Anglers of Western PA's 1st of 5 AOY qualifiers for the chapter.

This will also be our Signature event for 2026.

                                                                        

Entry fee:

KA member: $65 (TourneyX and Independent Judging fee included)

Non-member: $80 (TourneyX and Independent Judging fee included)

Optional $10 big bass buy in

Deadline to sign-up is Friday May 1st, at 8pm!

 

Event outline:

Friday May 1st

Virtual Captain's meeting via Facebook live will be at 8pm.

The 3-digit code will be announced live, emailed to you, and posted on the TourneyX app at 9pm. The 2026 KA universal identifier and code must be in all photos.

Reminder: Your Identifier must contain the 3-digit code, your initials, and must be completely visible in your fish submissions.

 

Saturday May 2nd

Launch and lines in: 6am

Lines out: 2pm

Fish submitted by: 2:30pm

Awards: 4pm

Awards Location: Sportsman's Warehouse

2075 Interchange Rd Suite 100 Erie, PA 16509

 

Sunday May 3rd

The 24-hour protest period ends at 4pm.

Winners will be paid via PayPal.

Please be considerate to state park users and other boaters on tournament day. Also, PLEASE follow the speed limit in the park! 25 MPH means 25 MPH!

An approved boundary map is available below and will be posted on TourneyX.

A safety flag is required to fish this event. If it's dark when you launch, you need a 360 light or headlamp.  

A Lake Erie permit in addition to your 2026 PA fishing license is also required.

You are not allowed to use a Donkey Leash, Fish Grips, or any other means of leashing the fish at this event. This is a requirement by PFBC and will result in you being disqualified from this tournament. Leave them in your car!

Limit: Your longest 5 bass in inches

2026 KA Universal Identifier: Download here or on the KAWPA Facebook page under the files tab

All KA rules apply. Please review the KA rules here: http://www.kayak-anglers.com/about/rules

Charity: Shriner's Children Hospital of Erie
